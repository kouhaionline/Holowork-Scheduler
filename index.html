Generate keeps earlier runs as history and regenerates from the chosen hour. To sync, expand an hour in the schedule and press Sync now: that hour becomes the current time and everything from that hour is regenerated starting now. Earlier hours are kept.

Schedule

Milestones: 1 / 5 / 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 300 / 400. Each run adds +1 to everyone in it. Members closest to their next milestone are picked first; on equal distance the earlier generation wins, then the earlier position in the list. Chosen members are listed in generation order. 🚫 ignores a member (not scheduled, not required to finish). Data is remembered in your browser. Exported files end with a #DATA line that lets import restore the starting counts, members and works – leave it in.
